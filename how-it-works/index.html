How it works

Compline scores the spot you just parked and can turn Tesla Sentry Mode on when that spot calls for it.

The score

Each park gets a score from 0 to 100. It uses local crime, parked-car crashes, and the time of day, along with context for the area: how busy it is, and how it is lit.

Quiet, Watchful, Elevated, Hot.

Those are the four color bands in the app. On the demo Now screen, the same kind of park is also written in plain language, such as “Medium risk.”

A score is an estimate from public data. It is not a promise that the car is safe, and it is not insurance.

Where it works

Early access covers Miami-Dade, Broward, and Palm Beach. Coverage inside those counties still depends on local records. Where a spot has no local data yet, Compline does not invent a calm score. It leans toward leaving Sentry on.

Auto-Sentry

Auto-Sentry is off until you turn it on. When it is on, Compline can switch Sentry on at a risky spot and off at a calm one. Home and quiet hours follow the schedule you set. You can turn it off again.

Changing Sentry can briefly wake the car. Compline is built to leave Sentry off when the spot does not call for it. That is what saves the battery. Sentry Mode itself is what draws power.

What Compline reads

Connecting a car uses Tesla sign-in. We never see your Tesla password. You approve a virtual key at tesla.com/_ak/getcompline.app so park data and Sentry changes can go through. Car data is sent only after you approve it for that vehicle.

After that, Compline reads park signals: location, gear, lock, Sentry state, and battery.

For parking, Compline turns Sentry Mode on or off. This page is not offering a remote for locks, climate, or driving.

Your latest park

The Now screen shows where you last parked. Compline is not a trip log.

Alerts

When you park, Compline can text you the score and whether Sentry is confirmed on. The text does not include the address. You see the spot in the app.

Questions

Is it beta?

Yes. Early access for South Florida Tesla owners. You are helping shape what we build.

Where does it work?

Miami-Dade, Broward, and Palm Beach. Inside that area, a spot with no local data yet does not get a made-up low score. Compline leans toward leaving Sentry on.

Does it drain my battery?

Sentry Mode is what draws the battery. Compline’s job is to leave Sentry off when the spot does not call for it. Turning Sentry on or off can briefly wake the car.

Can I turn it off or delete my account?

Yes. Auto-Sentry stays off until you turn it on, and you can turn it off in the app. You can remove the virtual key and revoke Compline in your Tesla account. You can delete the account from the account page, or email privacy@getcompline.app.
