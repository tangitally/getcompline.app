How it works

Compline scores every spot you park and turns Sentry Mode on when the spot calls for it.

The score

  • Local crime reports
  • Parked-car crashes
  • Area and foot traffic
  • Lighting
  • Time of day

38

Those inputs feed a score from 0 to 100. The app colors it Quiet, Watchful, Elevated, or Hot.

  • Quiet
  • Watchful
  • Elevated
  • Hot

The ring above is the demo Now screen: score 38, labeled Medium risk on that screen. It is a sample, not a typical park.

A score is an estimate from public data. It is not a promise that the car is safe, and it is not insurance.

Sentry, on and off

  1. Riskier spot

    Sentry turns on.

  2. Calmer spot

    Sentry turns off.

  3. Home

    On and off times you set. More saved places later.

Auto-Sentry stays off until you turn it on in Settings. You can turn it off again.

Connecting a car uses Tesla sign-in. We never see your Tesla password. You approve a virtual key at tesla.com/_ak/getcompline.app. Car data is sent only after you approve it for that vehicle.

After that, Compline reads park signals: location, gear, lock, Sentry state, and battery. The Now screen shows your latest park. Location history is kept for 90 days by default, and you can change that on your account page.

For parking, Compline turns Sentry Mode on or off. This page is not a remote for locks, climate, or driving.

Alerts

You get a text when you park. It includes the spot's score and Sentry status, not the address. You see the spot in the app.

If you allow it, the same alert can also arrive as a push notification.

Questions

Is it beta?

Yes. Early access in Miami-Dade, Broward, and Palm Beach.

Does it drain the battery?

Sentry Mode is what draws the power. Compline leaves it off where the risk is low. Turning Sentry on or off can briefly wake the car. The figures above are an example, not your car.

Can I turn it off or delete my account?

Yes. Auto-Sentry stays off until you turn it on, and you can turn it off in Settings. You can remove the virtual key and revoke Compline in your Tesla account. You can delete the account from the account page, or email privacy@getcompline.app.
