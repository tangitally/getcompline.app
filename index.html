What it does

Protection when it matters

Sentry turns on at riskier spots, not everywhere.

Less battery drain

Skip Sentry's drain where the risk is low.

Automatic or on your schedule

Let Compline decide, or set your own Home schedule.

Text alerts

A short text when you park, with the spot's score and Sentry status.

Estimated savings

Estimated battery and money saved if Sentry stays off for the hours in this example.

Parked 14 hours a day. 4 of those hours are treated as risky, so Sentry stays off for the other 10. The 4 hours are an example, not a measured Compline average.

Estimated · Battery

90 kWh a month

1,095 kWh a year

300 W for those 10 hours. 300 W is 7.2 kWh over 24 hours, the owner figure Electrek cited on 22 Feb 2024. Not a Tesla specification. The Model S owner's manual says power use may increase with Sentry on, and does not give watts.

Estimated · Money

$16.34 a month

$198.85 a year

90 kWh × 18.16¢/kWh. U.S. residential average, EIA Electric Power Monthly Table 5.3, August 2026 (preliminary).

Estimated · Range

360 miles a month

4,380 miles a year

90 kWh ÷ 0.25 kWh/mi. EPA lists 25 kWh/100 mi for the 2026 Tesla Model Y Long Range RWD.

Estimated · Supercharger time to put that energy back

21.6 minutes a month

262.8 minutes a year

90 kWh ÷ 250 kW × 60, at Tesla's published V3 peak of 250 kW. Real sessions are often slower, so this is a short estimate of the time.

3.00 kWh/day = 10 hours × 300 W ÷ 1,000

90 kWh/month = 3.00 × 30. 1,095 kWh/year = 3.00 × 365

$16.34/month from 90 × $0.1816 = $16.344. $198.85/year from 1,095 × $0.1816 = $198.852

360 miles/month = 90 ÷ 0.25. 4,380 miles/year = 1,095 ÷ 0.25

21.6 minutes/month = 90 ÷ 250 × 60. 262.8 minutes/year = 1,095 ÷ 250 × 60

3.36% of one EPA-rated full charge a day = 3.00 ÷ 89.25 kWh. 89.25 kWh = 357 miles × 0.25 kWh/mi. That is rated energy, not a pack size.

In February 2024, Tesla's Drew Baglino said the team was working to cut Sentry power by about 40%. This example does not apply that cut. Today's draw may be lower.
