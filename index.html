How it works Open app

Sentry when it matters. Battery when it doesn't.

Compline knows which parking spots are worth watching, and turns Sentry Mode on and off for you.

Join the Beta

Now open to Tesla owners in Miami-Dade, Broward and Palm Beach.

Set it once. Then just park.

  1. 1

    Connect your Tesla

    Sign in with Tesla. Then approve Compline in your Tesla app.

  2. 2

    Park like you always do

    Every time you park, Compline checks how risky that spot is, based on local break-in and damage reports.

  3. 3

    Sentry takes care of itself

    It goes on where your car needs watching and stays off where it doesn't. You get a quick text either way.

Savings

Left on around the clock, Sentry can use about 216 kWh a month. Compline keeps more of that for driving.
