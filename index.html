How it works

Three steps. Your Tesla account, your virtual key, your home pin. Fleet Telemetry streams park data to our server — not TeslaFi for testers.

  1. 1

    Sign in with Tesla

    OAuth with Tesla Fleet API. We never see your Tesla password. You pick which car.

  2. 2

    Add the virtual key

    In the Tesla app, open tesla.com/_ak/getcompline.app and approve Compline’s key. Required for Telemetry.

  3. 3

    Pin home & pick a mode

    Home is left out of scoring. Choose Relaxed, Balanced, or Cautious. Add a phone for park texts later.

What Compline will and won’t do

Designed to sit next to the Tesla app — dark, quiet, and honest about blank cities.

  • Automatic Sentry is off by default. You can turn Sentry on or off yourself from the app. Compline may briefly wake your car when needed for testing, and never sends other commands without your say-so.
  • Your data, your car. Location and Telemetry stay on our server for your account. Encrypted Tesla tokens.
  • Blank cities are honest. No crime feed yet means default Sentry ON until we have data — including Aventura today.
  • Revoke anytime. Remove the virtual key in the car’s Locks screen, or revoke Compline in your Tesla account.
