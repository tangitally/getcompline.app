What it does

Protection when it matters

Sentry turns on at riskier spots, not everywhere.

Less battery drain

Skip Sentry's drain where the risk is low.

Automatic or on your schedule

Let Compline decide, or set your own Home schedule.

Text alerts

A short text when you park, with the spot's score and Sentry status.
