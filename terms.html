Terms of use

These draft terms cover early (friend tester) access to Compline. Last updated: October 19, 2026.

The service

Compline scores the risk of where you park and helps you decide when Sentry Mode is worth the battery. If you turn on Automatic Sentry, it can also turn Sentry on for you. It is an independent product and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Tesla, Inc.

Eligibility and accounts

You must own or be allowed to control the Tesla you connect, and be able to authorize third-party apps in your Tesla account. One person per account. Testers join by invitation or from the waitlist. You must be at least 16.

Tesla authorization, virtual key, and Fleet Telemetry

  • You sign in with Tesla's own sign-in (Fleet API). Compline never asks for your Tesla password.
  • You pair Compline's virtual key (https://tesla.com/_ak/getcompline.app) on your car. Without it, telemetry and Sentry commands do not work.
  • Fleet Telemetry streams selected signals from your car, including its location, to Compline's server. Each car is configured only after you approve the list of signals; we do not configure cars silently.

Sentry, wakes, and commands

Automatic Sentry is off unless you turn it on. When on, Compline may turn Sentry on after you park. Compline's only car control is turning Sentry Mode on and off. That control may not be enabled at first (suggestions only). Compline may briefly wake your car to change Sentry, and does nothing else (at most 3 wakes a day, not below 20% battery). Compline never unlocks, opens, climate-controls, starts or drives your car. You remain responsible for your vehicle and for how you use Sentry Mode.

Location and home pin

You set a home pin so home parks can be handled separately (for example an overnight schedule you choose). Parking locations are processed to produce scores. Coverage varies by city; where we have no local data, Compline suggests leaving Sentry on.

Alerts

Alerts appear in the app and, if you allow it, as push notifications. Compline does not send text messages today; any future texts will be opt-in and covered by an update to these terms and the privacy policy.

Acceptable use

Do not abuse the service or the Tesla API, share sign-in codes, try to access another person's account or car, or reverse engineer telemetry credentials. Do not use Compline for emergencies or instead of your own judgment about safety.

Fees

Early tester access is free unless we tell you otherwise in advance. Tesla bills Fleet API usage per vehicle (data, wakes and commands); Compline currently absorbs it. Any charge to you will be disclosed before you are billed.

Disclaimer

Scores are estimates from public crime, crash and context data. They can be wrong, and a command can fail to reach the car. Compline is provided "as is" without warranties. To the fullest extent allowed by law, Compline is not liable for vehicle incidents, battery drain, missed or unwanted Sentry changes, or decisions you make about Sentry Mode.

Termination

You may stop anytime by turning off Automatic Sentry, revoking Compline in your Tesla account, removing the virtual key, and deleting your account. We may suspend access for abuse or to comply with Tesla's partner rules.
