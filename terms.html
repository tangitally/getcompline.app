Terms of use

These draft terms cover early (friend tester) access to Compline. Last updated: October 20, 2026.

The service

Compline scores the risk of where you park and helps you decide when Sentry Mode is worth the battery. If you turn on Automatic Sentry, it can also turn Sentry on for you. It is an independent product and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Tesla, Inc.

Eligibility and accounts

You must own or be allowed to control the Tesla you connect, and be able to authorize third-party apps in your Tesla account. One person per account. Testers join by invitation or from the waitlist. You must be at least 18.

Tesla authorization, virtual key, and Fleet Telemetry

  • You sign in with Tesla's own sign-in (Fleet API). Compline never asks for your Tesla password.
  • You pair Compline's virtual key (https://tesla.com/_ak/getcompline.app) on your car. Without it, telemetry and Sentry commands do not work.
  • Fleet Telemetry streams selected signals from your car, including its location, to Compline's server. Each car is configured only after you approve the list of signals; we do not configure cars silently.

Sentry, wakes, and commands

Automatic Sentry is off unless you turn it on. When on, Compline may turn Sentry on after you park. Compline's only car control is turning Sentry Mode on and off. You can turn Sentry on or off for your car from the app. Automatic Sentry is separate and off unless you turn it on. Compline may briefly wake your car to change Sentry, and does nothing else. Automatic Sentry and reply-ON wake the car at most 3 times a day, and never when the battery is below 20%. Compline never unlocks, opens, climate-controls, starts or drives your car. You remain responsible for your vehicle and for how you use Sentry Mode.

If you use WhatsApp or Telegram alerts, replying ON turns Sentry on for your car (same limits as Automatic Sentry). No other command can be sent by message. You're responsible for who can access your messaging account. If the battery is below 20%, the alert says so. For any other failure, the alert says Sentry couldn't be turned on.

Location and home pin

You set a home pin so home parks can be handled separately (for example an overnight schedule you choose). Parking locations are processed to produce scores. Coverage varies by city; where we have no local data, Compline suggests leaving Sentry on.

You are responsible for telling other people who drive the car that its location is shared with Compline.

Alerts and messages

Alerts appear in the app and, if you allow it, as push notifications. You can also choose WhatsApp, Telegram or email alerts in Settings. Each one is opt-in. Replying STOP to WhatsApp switches you back to app notifications. Compline does not send SMS text messages today. SMS is in carrier review. If we add SMS (planned provider AgentPhone, pending carrier approval), it will be opt-in with STOP and HELP, and we will update this page first. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Push alerts are delivered through your phone's or browser's own push service (for example Apple or Google).

No message includes an address, coordinates, or a place name. We will not add location to any message, now or later, without updating this page first.

WhatsApp uses a separate unchecked box at sign-up, not bundled with these terms. The box says: "Send me Compline parking alerts on WhatsApp at [number]. Reply STOP anytime." We store the time you opt in.

Acceptable use

Do not abuse the service or the Tesla API, share sign-in codes, try to access another person's account or car, or reverse engineer telemetry credentials. Do not use Compline for emergencies or instead of your own judgment about safety.

Fees

Early tester access is free unless we tell you otherwise in advance. Tesla bills Fleet API usage per vehicle (data, wakes and commands); Compline currently absorbs it. Any charge to you will be disclosed before you are billed.

Disclaimer

Scores are estimates from public crime, crash and context data. They can be wrong, and a command can fail to reach the car. Compline is provided "as is" without warranties. To the fullest extent allowed by law, Compline is not liable for vehicle incidents, battery drain, missed or unwanted Sentry changes, or decisions you make about Sentry Mode.

Termination

You may stop anytime by turning off Automatic Sentry, revoking Compline in your Tesla account, removing the virtual key, and deleting your account. We may suspend access for abuse or to comply with Tesla's partner rules.
